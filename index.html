The property

What is offered

The whole enterprise: the building, the land under it and a hotel that has been open since 1981.

The tower stands inside a quiet block behind Bulevardul Ștefan cel Mare, with its own forecourt, mature trees and a low entrance annex. It is connected to all utilities and heated by its own boiler house. The rooms, a 30-seat conference room and a 50-seat café are in daily use.

The building shows its age. That is the point: the frame, the plot and the location are sound, and the value lies in what a private owner can do with them.

Enterprise founded in 1992. 100% state-owned, administered by the Public Property Agency. IDNO 1003600080200.
